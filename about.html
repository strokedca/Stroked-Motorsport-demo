OUR STORY

About Stroked Motorsports Events

Built on Prince Edward Island for riders, clubs and motorsports organizers.

Why Stroked exists

Stroked Motorsports Events brings rides, car shows, motorsports gatherings, charity events and races into one accessible event calendar. The goal is to help people discover events and give organizers practical tools to plan attendance.

Basic event submissions, browsing and RSVPs are free.

Who is behind Stroked?

Stroked was started by Jason “Whisky” Lannigan, a Prince Edward Island motorcycle enthusiast and community organizer.

With roots in PEI and Atlantic Canada, the long-term goal is to connect motorsports communities across Canada.

Our approach

Clubs, charities and independent event hosts deserve an easy place to share accurate information. Every organizer remains responsible for their event details, cancellations and participant requirements.

Stroked is an independent event-listing platform—not the host of listed events.

Help build the calendar

If your group has a ride, show, fundraiser or competition coming up, submit it for approval.
