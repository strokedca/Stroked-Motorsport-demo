# Stroked promotion pricing proposal

Status: recommendations for owner review, not approved checkout prices. Public copy now explains duration pricing; no Stripe product, price or payment setting is changed by this proposal.

## Event upgrades

Use fixed campaigns of 2, 4 or 6 weeks (14, 28 or 42 days). Agree on the start and end dates before booking. Suggested launch prices in CAD, before any applicable tax:

| Optional upgrade | Scope | 2 weeks | 4 weeks | 6 weeks |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Featured Event | Featured badge and priority in relevant event listings | $15 | $25 | $35 |
| Enhanced Event Page | Up to six approved gallery photographs during the campaign | $10 | $15 | $20 |
| Sponsor Spotlight | Prominent sponsor name, optional logo and website link | $15 | $25 | $35 |

Each row is an independent optional upgrade. Basic event listings, standard event information, RSVP counts and ordinary sponsor credits stay free. Administrator promotions remain free regardless of duration.

## Business advertising

Use 1, 3 or 6 calendar months for ongoing business advertising. A calendar month is distinct from a four-week event campaign. Set explicit start and end dates on the booking. Suggested launch prices in CAD, before any applicable tax:

| Advertising option | Proposed scope | 1 month | 3 months | 6 months |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Business promotion card | Advertiser-supplied logo/photo, description and website link on the promotion board | $25 | $65 | $120 |
| Featured business promotion | Business card with priority placement on the promotion board | $50 | $135 | $250 |

Short business campaigns tied to a particular event can be quoted for 2, 4 or 6 weeks instead. Launch with fixed-term bookings, without automatic renewal. These proposed rates cover the agreed website placement and supplied artwork; additional design, social posts or email promotion need a separate agreed scope. Business-specific labels and paid fulfillment must be finalized before opening checkout for these products.

## Research checked on 2026-11-12

- [ValleyEvents.ca FAQ](https://mobile.valleyevents.ca/content/faq.php): Nova Scotia calendar lists a $10 featured poster for at least 30 days before an event, $25 per email banner/mailout, and a $25/month business ad. This is the closest regional low-cost comparison.
- [EventsToronto.ca promotion](https://eventstoronto.ca/promote-your-event): neighbourhood promotion lists $35/week or $120/four weeks plus HST; homepage promotion lists $69/week or $240/four weeks plus HST. These are launch rates for a different market and placement.
- [qathet365 promotion packages](https://qathet365.ca/promote/): event packages start at $300 for up to six weeks and include a custom Reel, Story shares, round-ups and a website banner. Extra promotion is listed at $50/week. This scope is broader than Stroked's proposed website-only upgrades.
- [BIC motorcycle event promotion](https://events.bicradio.com/promote): a motorcycle-specific example of free event listings with separate radio, social and newsletter promotion. The currently opened page does not publish numeric package rates; older search-result prices were excluded.

These are individual benchmarks, not a calculated market average. Audience sizes, services and placement differ. Stroked has no verified traffic figures in this review; review launch rates after collecting actual visits, advertisement views and clicks. Do not promise attendance, sales, exclusivity or off-site marketing that has not been agreed and implemented.

## Payment prerequisites

Public pricing in the site remains duration-based quotations. Obtain the owner's final prices and GST/HST registration status, finish the appropriate tax configuration, connect signature-verified Stripe payment events, and test checkout before collecting payments. Do not substitute historical demonstration prices.
