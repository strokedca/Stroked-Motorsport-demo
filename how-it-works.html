How Stroked Motorsports Events Works

Find an event, tell the organizer you are coming, or share an event of your own.

Four simple steps

01

Explore events

View the calendar and filter by available motorsport categories, locations and dates.

02

Read the listing

Check the organizer, start location, registration time, departure time, food and other posted details.

03

Choose your RSVP

Sign in with a rider account and select Going or Interested. You can update or remove your saved RSVP later.

04

Post your event

Organizers sign in, supply event details and submit the listing. It appears publicly after administrator approval.

Follow events and choose email alerts

Sign in and open an event to follow that event or its organizer. Your follows are saved to your account across devices. Under Account → Following & email alerts, you can separately choose newly approved events matching your regions and categories, updates from followed events and organizers, and day-before reminders for events you follow or RSVP Going / Interested.

Email choices start off. Save your choices to subscribe or stop emails. Reminders are sent from 9 a.m. Atlantic the day before. Text messages and push notifications are not available.

Stroked announcements

Under Account → Stroked announcements, you can separately subscribe to community news, site updates and occasional sponsor offers. Choose your announcement regions and motorsports interests. This subscription starts off and is separate from event alerts. Every announcement includes an unsubscribe link that works without signing in.

For organizers

Organizer accounts can submit events with a name, category, date, location, registration time, departure / kickstands-up time and any relevant stops, food or donation details.

You can attach an event poster, up to six photos, and sponsor logos. Use JPEG, PNG or WebP. Upload only images you have permission to use. Images are resized and become public only after the event or change is approved.

RSVP totals are planning estimates, not tickets or guaranteed attendance. Organizers remain responsible for confirming their event information.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost money to post an event?

No. Basic listings and RSVPs are free. Paid promotions remain paused pending the pricing review.

Can I change my response?

Yes. Sign in and return to the event to update or remove your RSVP.

Do I need an account to browse?

No. Browsing is available to visitors; saving shared RSVPs and organizer submissions requires an account.

Who confirms an event is running?

Contact the event organizer. The organizer controls event information and changes.
