Frequently asked questions

Does it cost money to post an event?

No. Basic listings and RSVPs are free. Paid promotions remain paused pending the pricing review.

Can I change my response?

Yes. Sign in and return to the event to update or remove your RSVP.

Do I need an account to browse?

No. Browsing is available to visitors; saving shared RSVPs and organizer submissions requires an account.

Who confirms an event is running?

Contact the event organizer. The organizer controls event information and changes.
