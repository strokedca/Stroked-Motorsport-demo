# Stroked Facebook promotion plan for morning review

Status: prepared for discussion. Nothing has been posted, no Facebook account or Page has been changed, and no paid campaign or budget has been authorized. Website advertising/upgrade packages remain paused.

## First goal

Promote the free Stroked event calendar to riders, clubs and event organizers. The first message should explain finding events, submitting an event for approval and choosing Going or Interested. Cover motorcycles, ATVs/off-road, classic cars, snowmobiles, and motocross/dirt track. Keep the paused advertising prices out of the launch message.

## Start with free promotion

1. Confirm the existing Stroked Motorsports Events Facebook Page, or plan a separate Page from the clothing business if needed. Add the approved events logo, a short description and the website link.
2. Prepare one clear launch post with the website link and a screenshot showing how to find or submit an event. Review the post with the owner before publishing.
3. Share selectively with relevant PEI, Nova Scotia and New Brunswick riding/motorsports groups when their rules or administrators permit it. Ask clubs and organizers to share the launch post. Do not mass-post or send automated invitations.
4. Follow with real event highlights and a short explanation of the free RSVP and organizer tools. Obtain permission for photographs. Avoid claims about existing audience size or guaranteed attendance.

## Optional paid test, after review

Choose a website-visits goal, suitable creative, a local audience and a fixed spending cap. Start with PEI; consider Nova Scotia and New Brunswick as separate later tests. Agree on the total budget, dates and creative before anything is activated. Compare website visits and actual event submissions/RSVP activity, rather than treating likes as completed registrations. Do not install tracking or promise conversion reporting that has not been configured.

The exact controls and available performance goals should be checked in the owner's current account. Meta's public training describes selecting a goal, creative, audience, budget/duration and placements; it does not establish the current options available to this particular account.
