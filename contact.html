Contact & Help

Questions about a listing, an account or the site? Contact Stroked.

Reach Stroked Motorsports Events

Strokedca@gmail.com

Email StrokedRead the Guide

Event corrections

Include the event name, date, organizer and details that need correcting. Organizers should use their account to submit changes where available.

Account assistance

Tell us which step failed—registration, confirmation, login or password reset—and include the error message. Never share a password, security code or payment-card number.

Questions about an event?

For ride routes, times, fees, cancellations, registration, conditions or eligibility, contact the event organizer shown in the listing. Stroked provides the calendar but does not operate those events.

Advertising and donations

Paid advertising and promotional pricing are paused for review. Voluntary site-support donations, when available, are not charitable donations and do not qualify for tax receipts.
