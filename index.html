How Stroked Events works

Find rides and motorsports events, share your event, and help organizers plan their turnout.

Explore events and use the calendar

Use Explore events to filter by category, year, province or territory, search words, or date range. Choose Month, Week or List. Tap a calendar date on a phone, or double-click it on a computer, to see that day’s events.

Open an event to check its location, registration time, kickstands up / departure time, route, food and organizer contact details.

What RSVP means — Going and Interested

RSVP means letting the organizer know your plans. Create a free rider account or sign in, open an approved event, then choose Going or Interested.

  1. Going: enter the number of additional people coming with you, if any.
  2. Interested: save your interest without being counted as going.
  3. Press Save RSVP. Your response is saved to your account and is available when you sign in on another device.

You can update your response or use Remove My RSVP. Totals are planning estimates. An RSVP does not buy a ticket or replace the organizer’s registration requirements.

Post an event — free organizer account
  1. Choose Organizer Login / Register and create or sign in to your organizer account. Confirm your email if requested.
  2. Complete your organizer profile, then choose Post an event.
  3. Add the event name, club, location, date, categories and ride details. Sponsor names and credits are free.
  4. Choose Submit event for approval. The listing becomes public after administrator approval.

To change or cancel a submitted event, open My account, find My events, and request the change or cancellation. Approved updates appear on the listing.

Featured Event, Enhanced Event Page and Sponsor Spotlight

Featured Event highlights a promotion and can give a linked event priority in relevant listings. Enhanced Event Page adds a photo gallery. Sponsor Spotlight gives a supporter a prominent name, optional logo and website link.

Event promotion prices are based on 2, 4 or 6 weeks. Business advertising is priced for 1, 3 or 6 calendar months. Open Advertise, choose a package and duration, and sign in to review your booking before opening Stripe checkout. Event upgrades require one of your approved events. Prices are one-time CAD payments with no GST/HST currently added and no automatic renewal. Check My account for verified payment and publication status. Stroked reviews promotions and supplied artwork before publishing them for the full booked duration.

What the Donation button is for

Donation is for voluntary help with running Stroked Events, including hosting, email and upkeep. It does not purchase an event upgrade, advertising or extra access. Donations toward an event or a charity are arranged with that event’s organizer separately.
