Motorsports Events We Cover

One calendar for multiple motorsports communities.

Five motorsports categories

Organizers can choose more than one category when an event fits multiple groups.

01

Motorcycles

Charity rides, poker runs, memorial rides, bike shows, group rides and motorcycle gatherings.

02

ATVs & off-road

Trail rides, ATV meetups, off-road outings and club events.

03

Classic cars

Show-and-shines, car shows, cruise nights and classic vehicle gatherings.

04

Snowmobiles

Snowmobile club rides, group outings and winter gatherings.

05

Motocross & dirt track

Motocross races, dirt track events, practice days and spectator meets.

Starting in Atlantic Canada

Stroked has PEI roots and is intended to grow beyond Atlantic Canada into other regions as organizers submit approved events. Coverage varies by area.
